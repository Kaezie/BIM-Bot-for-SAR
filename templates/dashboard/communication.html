MANUAL HEADSET TEST

Survivor communication

Live test required

Use the available headset microphone and earpiece on the robot, with a separate operator microphone and output at the laptop. Select all four devices explicitly. This opens two local audio routes; no audio is saved or sent to a cloud service.

The headset earpiece must be audible near the robot. Software readiness does not establish real range or audibility. Dashboard text stays on the responder's laptop; it is not sent to the survivor.

Two-way audio off. No microphone or output has been opened.

Setup and limitations

Connect or pair the headset with the laptop first. A wired headset is tethered. Use Google Chrome at localhost:5050 if output selection is unavailable in the in-app browser. Start with the robot and operator close together, then test the intended distance. Keep microphones away from their receiving speakers to reduce feedback. Some headsets mute themselves outside the ear or cannot support both inputs at once.

Discovery briefly requests microphone permission and releases the probe. Start opens only selected inputs; a mismatch or disconnect stops both directions. Hiding/closing the page or signing out stops audio. Two-way audio and the separate sound-analysis mode stop one another before starting.
