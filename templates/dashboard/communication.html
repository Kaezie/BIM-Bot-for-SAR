PROPOSED FEATURE

Survivor communication

Reply channel pending

The survivor can signal with gestures observed by the responder through the robot’s camera. To reply without a phone, the robot needs an output the survivor can perceive. A usable visible camera LED could provide simple light acknowledgements, if its presence and control are confirmed.

The team confirmed that survivors must communicate through the robot without a phone, and the robot has no speaker, buzzer, or display. Visible LED control is unconfirmed. Two-way communication is not available yet. Dashboard text stays on the responder’s laptop; it is not sent to the survivor.

How this could use the existing equipment

If your ESP32-CAM has an accessible visible LED, a firmware-controlled light acknowledgement could pair with camera-observed gestures. Light patterns need an agreed meaning that the survivor understands; they cannot explain arbitrary questions. Confirm the board and wiring before enabling this approach. Infrared illumination is not a visible message channel.

For spoken questions and replies, the planned microphone would need a robot speaker with a suitable driver or amplifier and audio firmware. That would require additional hardware. The current sound classification endpoint does not stream speech or interpret a survivor’s answer.
